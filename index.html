04 / Work Ethic & Team Values

How I Work,
What I Value.

What you can genuinely expect from me when I join your team or take on your project.

01 / PROBLEM SOLVING

Finding the actual cause, not just restarting the box.

If a server service freezes or a database query hangs, simply restarting it and hoping for the best doesn't solve anything. I take the time to check the event logs, inspect network traffic, and trace where the breakdown occurred. It takes a few extra minutes up front, but it prevents the same 2 AM alert from firing again the next night.

02 / UNDER PRESSURE

Steady and methodical when things get chaotic.

When airport biometric gates stall with hundreds of passengers waiting to board, or when trading servers trigger high-priority alerts before market opening, rushing around and panicking helps no one. I stay calm, follow troubleshooting checklists step by step, and keep colleagues updated so everyone knows what is being resolved.

03 / CONTINUOUS LEARNING

Honest about what I know, quick to learn what I don't.

I don't pretend to have mastered every technology under the sun. If an issue involves an unfamiliar tool or a specialized pipeline, I say so plainly, read through the technical documentation, ask the right questions, and figure it out. I take genuine responsibility for whatever system or task is handed to me.

A PERSONAL NOTE

What Colleagues and Team Leads Value in My Work

I believe in showing up on time, doing the unglamorous maintenance work thoroughly, and leaving systems in better shape than I found them. Colleagues I’ve worked with on shift know me as someone who listens before jumping to conclusions, doesn't bring ego into technical discussions, and stays dependable when the workload gets heavy.

Whether that means standing by at Terminal 2 until the final passenger boards or staying late to verify that a trading server cluster is healthy for tomorrow's shift, you can count on me to get the job done properly.
